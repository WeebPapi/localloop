# LocalLoop Vision

**Owner:** Aleksandre Kapanadze
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-11
**Update when:** the target customer, core value proposition, or product

## Why LocalLoop exists

Local businesses often share a neighborhood but do not have a simple, trusted
way to collaborate on customer acquisition and repeat visits. LocalLoop makes a
campaign between businesses legible: an advertiser offers a customer reward,
a nearby host helps a customer complete the qualifying action, and the value
flow is visible to everyone involved.

## Product thesis

LocalLoop should make local partnerships feel practical rather than promotional:

- **Advertisers** can run a bounded campaign and understand its cost and reach.
- **Hosts** can participate in a partnership with clear terms and a measurable
  outcome.
- **Customers** can discover a relevant reward and understand exactly how to
  unlock it.

The initial focus is a dense, relationship-driven Georgia pilot, beginning with
Tbilisi neighborhood businesses. We earn the right to broaden the product only
after the core loop works reliably for real participants.

## Product principles

1. **Make the exchange understandable.** Requirements, rewards, and business
   roles should be clear before a customer acts.
2. **Build for local trust.** The product should reinforce real relationships
   between nearby businesses rather than simulate an anonymous ad network.
3. **Prove one complete loop before expanding.** Campaign setup, host
   participation, customer progress, reward redemption, and outcome visibility
   matter more than marketplace breadth.
4. **Be truthful about money and infrastructure.** Demo funding is simulated;
   devnet activity is never presented as production settlement.
5. **Prefer operational clarity to feature volume.** A pilot team must be able
   to explain, support, and learn from every live flow.

## What LocalLoop is not, yet

- A general-purpose global loyalty marketplace
- A production payment, custody, escrow, or blockchain protocol
- A substitute for a business's POS, CRM, or authentication system
- A product that requires businesses to permanently choose only one role
